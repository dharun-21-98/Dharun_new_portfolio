# Personal Portfolio Website
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version:** 1.0

**Prepared For:** Antigravity

**Prepared By:** Dharunanand R

**Role:** Product Manager

**Document Purpose**
This document serves as the single source of truth for designing and developing my personal portfolio website. The objective is not merely to showcase my resume, but to communicate how I think, solve problems, collaborate with engineering teams, and deliver business value through technology.

The portfolio should leave visitors with one clear impression:

> This is someone who can own products from discovery to launch while understanding business, technology, AI, and execution.

---

# 1. Product Vision

The website should establish my professional identity as a modern Product Manager who bridges business strategy, engineering, and AI-driven product development.

Unlike traditional portfolio websites that function as online resumes, this platform should tell the story behind the products I have built, the decisions I have made, and the impact those decisions created.

The experience should be immersive, minimal, fast, and memorable.

---

# 2. Primary Goal

The primary objective is to convert recruiters and hiring managers into interview requests.

Every section should answer one of these questions:

• Who is he?
• What products has he built?
• How does he think?
• Can he work with engineering?
• Does he understand technology?
• Does he understand business?
• Is he someone we'd like to interview?

---

# 3. Success Metrics

The website should achieve the following:

- Visitors understand my profile within 30–45 seconds.
- Recruiters can access my resume within one click.
- Project case studies demonstrate ownership rather than task execution.
- Visitors spend time exploring projects instead of only downloading the resume.
- The website performs well on both desktop and mobile devices.
